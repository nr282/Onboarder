## Onboarder

## Introduction: The Onboarding Problem

As a developer at a major organization, I found that I spent 2 weeks configuring my development environment. The steps required were tedious, not well documented and time-consuming. However, these steps were discoverable and known to the organization, but distributed throughout docs and team members knowledge.

At the organization, I demonstrated that this process can be brought down from 2 weeks to less than 5 minutes by automating the process. The process becomes concrete, maintainable, fast and verifiable. The automation saved my current team 100k per annum. When scaled, the savings can be 10 million - 50 million dollars per annum in developer hours. Furthermore, it improves the speed of the organization. 

## Solution: Automation

The goal of this codebase is to provide an open source framework that will allow companies, individuals and others 
in open source to gain similar efficiency gains.
